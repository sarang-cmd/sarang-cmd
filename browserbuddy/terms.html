Using BrowserBuddy

BrowserBuddy helps you plan tasks, run a focus timer, choose sites to block, and optionally reflect on recorded activity. This version is provided without a user charge or in-app advertising. The standalone dashboard and extension have separate local data.

Important limitations

Blocking is limited to the installed Chromium-based browser and selected HTTP or HTTPS sites. Browser-restricted pages, service-worker-cached content, browser sleep or shutdown, extension disablement, and other devices or browsers can affect it. Pausing or ending a session removes the block, and a temporary allowance is available. Do not rely on it as a security, parental-control, emergency, or safety tool.

Activity recording is optional and approximate. A recorded visit is not proof of distraction or productivity. Title checks compare keywords and do not understand a page’s meaning. BrowserBuddy is not medical, therapeutic, educational, or professional advice.

Your content and external links

You decide which tasks, notes, links, and thumbnails to save. Keep backups of anything important. External websites and videos are controlled by their providers and may contain ads, recommendations, or their own terms. BrowserBuddy does not verify saved resources or guarantee ad-free playback.
